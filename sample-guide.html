Sample Guide — The Ember Road

Import it from the home window (Open the Ember Road Demo) or the Package Manager sample. The scene is generated by the shipped scene builder, so every link in it is real. Three quests, three achievements, one driver panel — the whole system on one screen.

The quests

  • First Embers (collect). Gather 3 ember shards → 50 experience → the linked achievement First Embers Complete.
  • The Gatekeeper's Price (wait-at-the-gate branch). Unlocks when First Embers completes. The gate holds until you speak to the gatekeeper; then the lever objective opens and grants a gate key.
  • Two Roads (route-immediately branch). Unlocks when The Gatekeeper's Price completes. Reputation ≥ 5 takes the warden's road; below that, the toll road (4 toll coins). Both roads rejoin at a reward node (100 currency) and the achievement Road Walker. A third achievement, Shard Hoarder, tracks 10 ember shards across your travels.

The driver

Six buttons, bottom-left, each calling the exact public API your game will call: Collect Ember Shard, Speak to the Gatekeeper, Pull the Gate Lever, Earn Reputation (+1), Defeat the Road Warden, Collect Toll Coin.

Two routes through Two Roads, both worth trying: finish the toll road as the demo routes you — then restart, press Earn Reputation five times before you finish The Gatekeeper's Price, and watch the branch take the warden's road instead.

The status panel

Top-center. It lists each quest's live state, the rewards granted so far, and the platform line, stated plainly on screen: platform sync runs on mock transports — unlocks are recorded locally, never sent to Steam or Epic.
