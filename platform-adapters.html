Platform Adapters

The honest page. Read it before you plan your Steam or Epic launch around this package.

What ships

  • An adapter layer: a Steam adapter and an Epic adapter over a transport interface (IPlatformTransport) that your code implements against your platform SDK.
  • A recording mock transport used to verify the layer.
  • A sync service that routes unlocks and progress reports to every registered adapter.

What is verified

The adapter logic is verified against the included mock harness: platform-id mapping (an achievement's Steam id reaches the transport; the Epic adapter falls back to the definition id when no Epic id is set), routing to all registered adapters, progress calls carrying id + current + target, and the no-transport behaviour — adapters report unavailable, calls return false, nothing throws. This evidence is mock evidence, and it is labelled that way everywhere: this page, the home window's Platform Sync section, and the demo's on-screen note.

What is not verified — and not claimed

No live Steam or Epic sync has been tested. No platform SDK is bundled and no native platform call is made by this package. Live verification needs your partner app ID and your SDK, which only exist in your project.

Going live

  1. Implement IPlatformTransport over your SDK: unlock and progress calls arrive with the platform achievement id resolved for you.
  2. Register the shipped adapters with your transport instead of the mock.
  3. Set platform ids on your achievement definitions (Steam id, Epic id; the definition id is the fallback).
